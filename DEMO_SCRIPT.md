# SplitMind — 2-minute demo video script (English)

Record real screen footage of https://splitmind-bd.vercel.app at 1080p. Read the voiceover at a calm pace; each block is roughly the time shown. Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** (or Public) and paste the link in the submission form.

Suggested title: **SplitMind — AI bill splitter for Bangladesh | YUNet AIgnite Buildathon 2026**

---

**0:00–0:15 · Problem** — *Show the home page.*

> "After a dinner or a trip, shared expenses end up scattered across group chats — often in Banglish. Working out who owes whom, with uneven participation and old debts, is slow and awkward. SplitMind fixes that."

**0:15–0:30 · Group** — *Type Rahim, Karim, Sadia. Open "Payment details" on one person to show bKash.*

> "I add the people who shared the costs. Payment details like bKash or Nagad are optional."

**0:30–1:00 · AI extraction (the key moment)** — *Expenses → "Use notes instead" → paste the text below → "Extract with AI".*

```
Rahim dinner er bill dise 1500 taka, sobai share korbo. Karim uber e 450 dilo amra 3 jon. Sadia cinema ticket 900 but Karim jai nai. Karim Rahim ke ager 200 dhar.
```

> "Instead of typing every bill, I paste the chat exactly as it was written. Gemini reads the Banglish and builds a structured ledger: three bills and one old debt. Notice it understood 'Karim jai nai' — Karim didn't go — so he's left out of the cinema ticket."

**1:00–1:15 · Human review** — *Scroll through the review cards; change one checkbox and change it back.*

> "AI proposes, I confirm. Every payer, amount and participant is editable, and nothing reaches the plan until I continue."

**1:15–1:40 · Settlement** — *Continue to settlement. Point at the two transfer cards. Open "Paid part of this amount?" and "Write reminder".*

> "SplitMind calculates in exact poisha and finds the fewest transfers: Karim pays Rahim 400 taka, Sadia pays Rahim 200. I can record a partial payment, write a reminder in English, Bangla or Banglish, or hand off on WhatsApp. SplitMind never touches the money itself."

**1:40–1:52 · Receipt (optional)** — *If you have a permitted receipt photo: "Add a bill photo" → read with AI → show the total and VAT.*

> "Receipts work too — Gemini vision reads the total, VAT and service charge, with on-device OCR as a fallback."

**1:52–2:05 · Share + close** — *Click "Copy plan link", then show the Group trip example from "Try an example".*

> "I can share the plan as a link, print or export it. SplitMind is free, needs no sign-up, and is live now at splitmind-bd dot vercel dot app. Thank you."

### Recording tips
- Close the Quick guide pop-up before recording, and use a clean browser window at 100% zoom.
- If AI ever shows "busy", wait a few seconds and press Extract with AI again — the server now retries and falls back to other Gemini models automatically, so this should be rare.
- Only show real results. Do not describe the local fallback or the fictional examples as live AI.
