# Paying through SplitMind

1. In Group, open the recipient’s Payment details and choose bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay or another method.
2. Add the recipient’s actual wallet/reference number and optionally their provider-issued HTTPS payment link or QR. Never enter PINs, OTPs, card details or invented URLs.
3. In Settle, expand Payment options and choose the method. Copy the amount, number or combined instructions.
4. If a link exists, open it. Check its hostname, receiver, amount and provider fees. It may open the browser or provider app depending on the link/device. A recipient-provided URL is not verified or endorsed by SplitMind.
5. Without a link, open the provider app yourself. Use Send Money for a personal wallet, or Payment for a merchant as supported by the provider. Check receiver and amount independently. A phone number alone does not generate an official transfer link.
6. Complete payment externally, then return to mark the full amount or record a partial payment. Opening links, viewing QR, and copying text do not verify or record a transfer.

No provider credentials or payment gateway account are needed for these external handoff tools. Actual provider transaction fees may apply. SplitMind does not hold funds, process card/crypto transactions, exchange currencies or verify payments. Bank/card/crypto references follow the same manual approach. QR uploads remain session-only; wallet numbers and links are included in optional saved drafts and portable backups.

The combined ZIP contains all v1.9.1 source and deployment instructions. Replace source in the existing GitHub repository connected to Vercel. Redeploying old source alone will not add these changes. No AI API variables are required.
