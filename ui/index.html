About SplitMind

SplitMind helps friends, families and travel groups split shared bills and see who pays whom. Enter people, add bills, review the amounts and share the settlement plan.

Calculations use BDT and equal shares for the people selected on each bill. SplitMind is a bill calculator and record keeper; it does not hold, send or receive money.

How it works
  1. Add the people sharing the bills.
  2. Add a bill, write notes, or choose a bill photo.
  3. Check the amount, payer and participants.
  4. Follow the settlement plan. Record payments after paying.

Amounts are in BDT. Bills are shared equally among the people you select.

Privacy

Your entries stay in this browser unless you choose AI or a payment service. Saving is optional and local to this device. Photos, access codes and payment verification are not saved. Delete saved drafts through “Save or restore”.
