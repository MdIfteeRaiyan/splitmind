About & help
About SplitMind

SplitMind helps friends, families and travel groups split shared bills and see who pays whom. Enter people, add bills, review the amounts and share the settlement plan.

Calculations use BDT, with equal shares or custom amounts for each selected person. SplitMind is a bill calculator and record keeper; it does not hold, send or receive money.

How it works
  1. Add the people sharing the bills.
  2. Add a bill, write notes, or choose a bill photo.
  3. Check the amount, payer and participants.
  4. Follow the settlement plan. Record payments after paying.

Amounts are in BDT. Choose equal shares or enter a custom amount for each person.

Manual bills, limited English/Banglish notes, equal/custom splits, payment records and sharing run on this device. Optional Gemini/Groq AI requires server configuration and is subject to provider quotas and data terms. Manual tools remain available. Receipt OCR uses Tesseract.js (Apache-2.0), downloaded from jsDelivr with language data from Project Naptha. OCR needs internet to load, can be slow and must be checked. Payments happen outside SplitMind and are never provider verified.

Privacy

Your entries stay in this browser unless you choose AI, share, export or open a payment service. AI sends selected content to the configured provider. Shared snapshot links contain names and amounts; anyone with the link can read them. Do not post private plans publicly. OCR downloads third-party software and models; those hosts receive ordinary download requests, not your receipt image. Saving is optional and local to this device. Photos, access codes and full-payment marks are not saved. Manual payment adjustments are saved with the ledger only when saving is enabled. Delete saved drafts through “Save or restore”.
